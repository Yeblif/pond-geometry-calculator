05 — Freeboard and capacity basis

m

Freeboard is measured vertically below the lowest rim elevation (overflow point), not the highest rim. Water is always horizontal. Where the base or pond sides rise above the water level, they store no water.

Model: a planar ground (rim) surface and planar base with independent X/Y grades. Depth is measured at plan centre. Sides drop from their closest top-rim point at the specified inward slope, meeting the base where possible. The pond cannot store water above its lowest rim point; freeboard is below that controlling spill elevation. Maximum storage is not an earthworks excavation quantity. Numerical grid integration and rendered meshes are approximate; finite sampling can affect narrow shapes and steep slopes. Flat zero-gradient results correspond to a conventional level-rim pond. No outfall, berm/retaining-wall design, ground-condition, hydraulic routing, sediment or operational storage allowance is included. Concept sizing only; verify detailed levels, stability, and volumes in CAD.
