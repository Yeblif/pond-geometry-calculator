Assumptions: level top rim and base; constant inward side slope; uniform excavation depth; no irregular ground, benching or curved batter transitions. Area means the top plan area, not wetted surface or land area. Total volume is the geometric capacity to the rim. Usable volume is the pond capacity below the freeboard level (does not account for sediment, permanent dead storage, or operational allowances). Circular sides are conical; square/rectangular sides are planar. Custom outlines use a numerical distance-to-rim model; results are approximate and narrow areas can taper to zero before reaching the design base. This is a conceptual sizing tool, not an earthworks design check.
